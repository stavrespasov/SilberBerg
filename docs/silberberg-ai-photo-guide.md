# Silberberg AI photography brief

## Purpose

This is the production brief for the twelve images that will replace the blank gallery frames on the Silberberg website. It is written for manual use in ChatGPT Image / GPT Image.

The website’s visual language is **Midnight Vault**: warm near-black surfaces, restrained old-world luxury, real materials, thin gold accents, soft directional light, and editorial rather than glossy advertising photography. The photos should feel like they belong to the same quiet, precise collection.

There are four image slots per category. Every category page displays the images in a 2 × 2 gallery with a **4:5 portrait crop**. The first photo in each category is also displayed on the homepage in a **4:3 landscape crop**. This is the key constraint: the first image needs room around its subject on both the left/right and top/bottom so it remains strong in either crop.

Do not add text, logos, watermarks, price tags, labels, borders, collages, UI elements, or decorative graphics to any generated image.

## Recommended generation workflow

1. Generate one image at a time with the exact prompt for its slot.
2. Pick the strongest result before generating the next image. Keep the mood and lighting consistent across the category.
3. Ask for a portrait-oriented image with the product fully inside a central safe area. A source image at least 2048 px tall is preferable.
4. Save the chosen images with the exact filenames in the asset table below. Use `.webp` if your export process supports it; otherwise use high-quality `.jpg` and I will optimise/convert them when you return them.
5. Send the selected images back without cropping them aggressively. I will apply the final responsive crops and check them on desktop, tablet, and phone.

## Shared visual direction — include in every prompt

Use this paragraph as the common style layer for every image prompt below. It is already included in the detailed prompts, but it is useful for revisions.

> Photorealistic editorial product photography for a discreet European precious-metals buyer. Midnight Vault visual language: near-black charcoal or very dark warm-brown setting, restrained old-world luxury, soft directional studio light from the upper left, controlled highlights, deep but readable shadows, fine material texture, muted warm gold and antique-silver tones, quiet premium mood, realistic imperfections, shallow to moderate depth of field, no people, no faces, no hands, no text, no logos, no branding, no watermarks, no CGI look, no excessive gloss, no bright white background, no saturated colours.

### Composition rules

- **Gallery crop:** all twelve images will be shown at 4:5. Keep the main subject inside the middle 70% of the frame and leave breathing room around it.
- **Homepage crop:** the first image in each category must also survive a 4:3 crop. Keep its main subject central and avoid important details at the very top/bottom edges.
- **People:** prefer product-only imagery. Do not generate any person, hand, face, reflection of a face, mannequin head, or partial face.
- **Materials:** show genuine texture: small scratches on metal, natural fur fibres, leather grain, dust or patina on older objects. Avoid immaculate showroom plasticity.
- **Lighting:** one controlled warm key light, with shadows that remain legible against the dark site. Avoid black-on-black where the product silhouette disappears.
- **Colour:** the image should work beside `#0B0B0D` black, bone-white typography, and muted gold accents. Bronze, amber, old silver, oxblood brown, tobacco brown, charcoal, and muted cream are appropriate.
- **No text:** this is important. Product marks are okay only if they are generic and unreadable. Avoid recognisable brand names entirely.

## Revision prompts

Use these after a generation only when needed. Keep the original prompt and add one focused correction.

### If the item is too close to an edge

> Recompose the image with the complete product placed inside the central 70% of the frame, leaving more dark space on all sides for responsive 4:5 and 4:3 crops. Keep every other visual detail unchanged.

### If it looks too commercial or glossy

> Reduce the advertising polish. Use more realistic fine scratches, natural material variation, lower-sheen highlights, and a quieter private-appraisal mood. Keep the object, composition, and dark studio setting unchanged.

### If a person or face appears

> Remove every person, head, face, hand, skin area, reflection, and mannequin feature. Keep only the product on an invisible garment form or dark studio surface. Preserve the composition and lighting.

### If the background is too bright

> Replace the background with a near-black charcoal or dark warm-brown studio setting. Preserve controlled warm highlights on the product, but keep the edges and background dark enough to sit beside a black website.

### If branding or text appears

> Remove all logos, brand names, monograms, readable markings, labels, text, and watermarks. Replace them with natural unbranded material texture while preserving the product’s believable form.
